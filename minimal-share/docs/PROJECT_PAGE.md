JShare discovers nearby Android devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot and transfers files directly over the LAN. It supports files, photos, videos, installed applications, single APK packages, and Split APK bundles.

当前版本无需账号、不依赖云端服务器，适合在自己的 Wi‑Fi 或手机热点环境中快速互传文件与应用。
